In a world chasing immediacy, we look for cadence. In a system built for scale, we return to proportion. Design is not about decorating profit but about negotiating meaning between people, material, and time. Use is not the only value. Process is also a form of integrity. Restraint is also a form of clarity. We build for utility, but stay open to the unseen.

Hami means 'we'. It’s a small word, but it carries a deep sense of togetherness and purpose. We’re a design studio and storytelling lab, founded in 2025, shaped by two distinct rhythms: Mumbai, with all its energy, and Kalimpong, with its quiet altitude. We move between the two on purpose—working at the intersection of momentum and stillness. For us, design is not an afterthought but the invisible architecture beneath an idea—shaping how people connect, behave, and inhabit a space or story. Brand systems, user experiences, and narratives are threads of the same fabric, woven into a cohesive whole. We partner with organizations from the outset, working through the logic of a vision to lay its foundation—because lasting work begins long before it is seen. Therefore, we believe that design should be: ➀Playful— bringing delight in ways that feel both unexpected and intentional. ➁ Contextual rather than trend-driven— every challenge is specific and deserves a solution rooted in its own environment. ➂ Impactful— adding meaning and lasting value to everyday interactions. ➃ Conversational rather than just communicative— sparking dialogue instead of simply sending a message. ➄ Ever-evolving— the best work is never finished, only refined.

Built To
Collaborate

For every project, we build teams of designers, developers, writers, and researchers united by curiosity and a deep care for the work. Our partnerships are often long-term, grounded in shared purpose, trust, and mutual respect. We are especially drawn to collaborators who move between disciplines and explore the edges of their fields. If your practice sits at the intersection of design, research, storytelling, technology, or something much harder to name, we would love to connect.

Our Practice
At Hami, we are deeply invested in our process—but not at the cost of well-being. We choose to work a five-day week, not as a constraint, but as a commitment to sustaining clarity, energy, and attention in everything we do. This balance nurtures not just our practice, but our lives beyond it. Each project is approached with care and intention, and we’re transparent about the time, experience, and thoughtfulness required to do meaningful work. While our fees reflect this depth, we remain open to collaborating with early-stage teams, individuals, or collectives whose values align with ours. Where there is clarity of purpose and mutual respect, we’re always open to a conversation.
